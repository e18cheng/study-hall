# Study Hall Roadmap

The app opens on a home page with a card for each live topic. Deep links (`#world`, `#presidents`, `#shakespeare`, `#elements`, `#wars`) survive a refresh, and the Home item in the menu or the Study Hall brand in the header returns to the picker.

## Modules

### 1. World Capitals
- Live now (inside World Geography): country → capital and capital → country typing quizzes, capitals in the study list and map tooltip, and separate capital progress. Capitals cover sovereign countries only.
- Next: click the capital's country on the map, a capital weak-spot drill, and capital progress on the home card.
- Extensions: hard mode (tricky capitals like Canberra, Brasilia), list by region

### 2. US States and Capitals
- Click the state on the map, or type its name
- State to capital, and capital to state
- Extensions: nicknames, admission order, largest cities that are not capitals

### 3. Rivers, Mountains, Deserts, and Lakes
- Click the feature on a map
- Extensions: superlatives (longest, tallest, largest), which countries it touches

### 4. British Monarchs and World Leaders
- Ordered recall, like the presidents quiz
- Extensions: reign years, houses/dynasties, prime ministers

### 5. Shakespeare and Literature
- Play to character, author to work, work to author
- Extensions: famous opening lines (paraphrased), literary prizes, poets
- Live now: play ↔ character and synopsis → play flashcards, plus the full plays guide and list drill. Plays can be marked memorized, and the flashcard pool can be limited to them.

### 6. Science
- Live now: a Periodic Table module with all 118 elements, category colors, group/period labels, lanthanide and actinide rows, a tap-to-study detail card, and three quizzes (symbol → name, name → symbol, and find the element on the table). Pools cover All, First 20, Common 40, Metals, Nonmetals, Noble gases, and Metalloids; progress and best scores are separate from other topics.
- Next: element → atomic number, category and group drills, and a weak-spot review.
- Extensions: famous scientists and their discoveries, SI units, human body, planets and moons

### 7. Arts and Music
- Composer to famous works, painter to paintings
- Extensions: opera, ballet, architecture, art movements

### 8. Mythology and Ancient History
- Greek/Roman god equivalents, myths to characters
- Extensions: Egyptian and Norse gods, ancient empires and rulers

### 9. Nobel and Pulitzer Winners
- Year/category to winner
- Extensions: Peace Prize, Oscars Best Picture

### 10. Word Origins and Wordplay
- Etymology items
- Extensions: Before and After puzzles, common prefixes and roots

### 11. US Wars
- Live now: a study timeline grouped by era, with years, opponent, president(s), outcome, and a short summary for each conflict. Pools are Core 12 and All conflicts (Core 12 plus the French and Indian, Northwest Indian, Barbary, Philippine–American, and Cold War). Four quizzes cover war → years, years → war, war → president, and war → opponent; the president quiz accepts any president who served during the war, and the opponent quiz accepts aliases. Progress and best scores are separate from every other topic.
- Next: battle and treaty drills, a weak-spot review, and a "which war came first" ordering quiz.
- Extensions: causes and consequences, key generals, home-front timeline, and a map of each war's theaters.
