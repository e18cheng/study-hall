# Study Hall Roadmap

## Modules

### 1. World Capitals
- Country to capital, and capital to country
- Click the capital's country on the map
- Extensions: hard mode (tricky capitals like Canberra, Brasilia), list by region

### 2. US States and Capitals
- Click the state on the map, or type its name
- State to capital, and capital to state
- Extensions: nicknames, admission order, largest cities that are not capitals

### 3. Rivers, Mountains, Deserts, and Lakes
- Click the feature on a map
- Extensions: superlatives (longest, tallest, largest), which countries it touches

### 4. British Monarchs and World Leaders
- Ordered recall, like the presidents quiz
- Extensions: reign years, houses/dynasties, prime ministers

### 5. Shakespeare and Literature
- Play to character, author to work, work to author
- Extensions: famous opening lines (paraphrased), literary prizes, poets

### 6. Science
- Periodic table: symbol to element, element to number
- Famous scientists and their discoveries
- Extensions: SI units, human body, planets and moons

### 7. Arts and Music
- Composer to famous works, painter to paintings
- Extensions: opera, ballet, architecture, art movements

### 8. Mythology and Ancient History
- Greek/Roman god equivalents, myths to characters
- Extensions: Egyptian and Norse gods, ancient empires and rulers

### 9. Nobel and Pulitzer Winners
- Year/category to winner
- Extensions: Peace Prize, Oscars Best Picture

### 10. Word Origins and Wordplay
- Etymology items
- Extensions: Before and After puzzles, common prefixes and roots
